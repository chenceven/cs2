PRACTICE 02 / 03

Economy

Money is the only resource the whole team shares. Spend it alone and you have bought yourself a rifle and your team a disadvantage.

What it means

THE IDEA IN ONE BREATH

Every round you either buy something that helps the team or you keep the money for a round where it can. The mistake beginners make is the half-buy: five hundred credits spent on a pistol and a dream, which leaves the team unable to full-buy next round and unable to win this one either.

The fix is not complicated. One person calls the buy. Everyone follows it. If the call is "save", nobody is a hero with an SMG.

How I practise it

THREE RULES, NOT ONE HUNDRED

  • Rule one One caller, one buy, no side deals
  • Rule two Full-buy above four thousand, save below
  • Rule three After a lost buy round, save one round on purpose
  • Daily drill Say the number out loud before the freeze ends
  • What good looks like Five players with the same gun, not one with a good one
