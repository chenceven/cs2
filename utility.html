PRACTICE 03 / 03

Utility

A grenade is not a decoration. Either it moves somebody where you want them, or it was a present you gave to the other team.

What it means

THE IDEA IN ONE BREATH

Utility is the cheapest way to change a fight without changing your aim. A smoke makes a corridor one-way. A flash makes a player look away for a second and a half. A molotov moves someone out of a corner they were counting on.

The mistake is quantity: five flashes thrown at the same wall, none of them timed with a teammate moving. One flash thrown as someone steps out is worth more than all five.

How I practise it

THREE RULES, NOT ONE HUNDRED

  • Rule one One grenade, one purpose — say it before throwing
  • Rule two Throw for the teammate who is actually moving
  • Rule three Keep one back for the retake, not for the entrance
  • Daily drill Ten minutes in a private server on one map, two line-ups
  • What good looks like The enemy is somewhere they did not choose
