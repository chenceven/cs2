The game, in one paragraph

WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS

Counter-Strike 2 is a free-to-play tactical shooter on Steam, released in September 2023 on the Source 2 engine. Five players against five: the Terrorist side tries to plant the bomb, the Counter-Terrorist side tries to stop them or defuse it. Rounds are short, money carries over, and one careless peek can cost the whole round.

What keeps me coming back is not the shooting. It is that almost every round can be explained afterwards. Why did we lose that 3v2? Was the money spent badly two rounds earlier? Did we give away our position with a sound nobody noticed? The game always has an answer, and it is usually mine.
